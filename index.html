The Tastes of Malaysia

Three dishes. Three stories. Truly a nation that takes it's food very seriously.

Laksa

A fiery, soul-warming noodle soup that defines Malaysian street food. Thick rice noodles swim in a rich coconut milk broth — or a tangy tamarind-based one in Penang — layered with prawns, fish cakes, tofu puffs, and a crown of fresh herbs. Each state claims its own definitive version, and every bowl starts an argument.
